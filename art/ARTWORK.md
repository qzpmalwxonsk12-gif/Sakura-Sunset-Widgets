# Sakura Sunset artwork

The landscape is original, generated specifically for this project using the built-in image-generation tool. The unmodified source is `source/sakura-landscape.png`. No downloaded stock art or external runtime assets are used.

`tools/generate_assets.py` samples it onto a 320 × 180 grid with nearest-neighbor resampling, creates a fixed 96-color background, adds deterministic pixel petals, and writes 96 losslessly indexed PNGs plus unfiltered Android bitmap drawable wrappers. Soft, five-pixel corner cutouts are baked into the alpha mask. The sky, tree, landscape and sun remain stationary; there is deliberately no secondary animation in version one.

The petal paths are periodic over 96 frames / 12 seconds. Each petal has its own phase, release site, wind travel, vertical descent, oscillation, color and rotation. Paths finish outside the painting before their next release. There is no duplicate terminal frame. GIFs are development previews only; Android displays the PNG resources.

## Exact generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original background plate for a premium pixel-art animated Android home screen widget, Sakura Sunset. Create a polished 16:9 landscape illustration, full bleed, ideally 1536x864. Scene composed on a coherent low-resolution 320x180-like pixel grid: crisp square pixels, deliberate clustered shading and restrained dithering, like a beautiful artisanal indie game environment, absolutely no blur or painterly strokes. Foreground lower quarter: gently curved grassy hill, deep teal and forest green shadow masses with sparse golden moss grass highlights and little pink fallen blossom pixels. Main subject slightly left of center: a magnificent old Japanese sakura tree, dark twisting trunk rooted at x=0.36 y=0.80, gnarled graceful curved branching visible through an asymmetrical cloud of finely textured pink blossoms. Canopy occupies x=.12 to .60, y=.15 to .56; irregular layered sprays and natural silhouette, absolutely not circles, dark mauve undersides, coral pink middle, delicate peach and pale blush highlights on right upper edges. Background: atmospheric layered rolling Japanese mountains in muted violet and lavender; valley haze, warm horizon. Sky top muted purple and dusty rose, fading through pink to peach/coral and warm orange at horizon. A pale golden setting sun centered at x=.76 y=.54, diameter .13 of width, partly grazed by far mountains. A few thin long lavender/coral sunset clouds, subtle. Overall serene nostalgic warm luminous sunset balanced with cool dark hill. Keep whole tree and sun within central 80 percent of image. NO flying petals in sky: these will be animated separately; only static blossoms on canopy and ground. No text, branding, frame, border, UI, buildings, people, watermark. Masterful pixel-art composition, detailed but readable at tiny size.
